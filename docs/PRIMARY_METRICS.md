# Primary metrics - pre-registered (Phase 1.5). Written and committed BEFORE any detector/policy was run on held-out set v1.

Population: held-out v1 (`data/heldout/heldout_v1.jsonl`): 78 flood traces (9 structures x 8 seeds + 6 hand-written) and
122 legitimate traces (11 structures x 10 seeds + 12 hand-written). All SYNTHETIC. Ground truth = `ground_truth` field.

## Terms
* **Call index** `i`: 0-based position in emission order across the whole trace (`gidx`).
* **Flag** (detector-level): a single detector's `DetectorResult.flag` on a whole trace. `first_index` = call index at which it fires.
* **Policy** = a fixed rule that combines detectors and/or a cap (defined in `docs/SELECTION_PROCEDURE.md`).
* **Intervention** (policy-level): the policy would cancel the current generation at call index `i*` = the minimum `first_index`
  over the policy's components. For the per-generation cap `C`, `i*` is the index of the (C+1)-th call of a generation. The call at
  `i*` and everything after it is NOT executed; calls `0..i*-1` were allowed. A policy either intervenes (at `i*`) or never does on that trace.
* Detectors are evaluated offline on whole traces with their causal definition (a detector only uses calls `<= first_index`), so
  offline `first_index` equals the index an online guard checking after every completed call would use, EXCEPT `cross_turn`, which is
  evaluated at generation end offline (online it could fire earlier on a partial batch). Not evaluated online in Phase 1.5.

## The four primary metrics (reported for every selectable policy AND every diagnostic detector)
1. **Flood detection rate** = (# flood traces with an intervention/flag) / 78. An intervention that occurs at an index BEFORE
   the objective `loop_start_index` still counts as detected, but is counted separately as "premature" and listed.
2. **False-positive rate** = (# legitimate traces flagged) / 122, per detector (flag) and per policy (intervention). Per trace, not per call.
3. **Detection latency**, for detected floods:
   * `latency_after_start = max(0, i* - loop_start_index)` (calls wasted after the loop objectively began), and
   * `latency_total = i*` (calls allowed before intervention).
   Reported as median, IQR and max across detected floods, plus the count of undetected floods (never intervened = infinite latency;
   they are false negatives and are NOT dropped from the medians silently: a second "median with misses counted as trace length"
   is reported).
4. **Legitimate task interruption rate** = (# legitimate traces where the policy intervenes at any point, i.e. the task could not
   complete as run) / 122. For a policy this equals its false-positive rate by definition; it is reported separately for clarity and also
   as the mean fraction of the legitimate trace's calls not executed = mean of `(n_calls - i*)/n_calls` over interrupted traces.

## Confidence intervals
Rates: **Clopper-Pearson exact two-sided 95%** (used for selection) and Wilson 95% (also shown). Latency: percentile bootstrap (2000
resamples, fixed seed 20260929) for the median. Caveat stated in advance: traces within a structure are generated by the same
template and are NOT independent, so the intervals understate real uncertainty about performance on structures we did not think of.
Per-structure detection/FP tables are therefore always shown next to pooled numbers.

## Reporting of errors
Every false positive and every false negative is listed for (a) the selected policy (or, if none qualifies, the primary candidate
`S0`) and (b) every diagnostic detector's false positives are counted and tabulated per structure. Full per-trace, per-candidate
outcomes go to `results/phase1_5/per_trace_outcomes.csv`. Each listed error gets: trace id, structure, `first_index`/`loop_start`,
and an explanation of why the detector did or did not fire (mechanism, written after the fact and labelled as such).
Post-hoc explanations are labelled as post-hoc and are not used to change anything (see selection procedure).
